Reading Tracker

Roadmap

What the app does today, and what's coming next. Many of these ideas came from you, so thank you!

Available now

In the app

Set a reading goal and the app tells you how much to read each day.

  • Reading goalsA book, its page count, an optional start page, and the date you want to finish.
  • Book detailsTitle, author, ISBN, language and year for each book.
  • Finished booksA tab with every book you've finished, by year. Mark a book as finished yourself, or let the app do it on the last page. Read it again any time.
  • Daily targetPages left, days left, and how many pages to read today.
  • Easy loggingLog the pages you read or the page you stopped at, also for earlier days.
  • Backup & restoreSave your data to a file, restore it any time, or back up to a folder automatically.
  • English & GermanPlus a dark mode that follows your phone.
  • Updates inside the appMost improvements arrive without reinstalling.

Stay motivated

Planned

See how your reading adds up.

  • Reading chartsPages per week and per month, with your average.
  • Reading streaksHow many days in a row you've read, and your record.
  • Ahead or behind?See at a glance whether you're on track for your finish date.
  • Quick-log buttonsAdd +10 or +20 pages with one tap.

Your books

Planned

More than page numbers: your books, your notes, your next read.

  • Notes & quotesKeep your thoughts and favorite lines with each book.
  • To-be-read listCollect the books you want to read, and start a goal from one when you're ready.
  • What should I read next?Can't decide? Let the app pick a book from your list.
  • momox linkFind a book from your list at momox.
  • Share your progressSend your week's pages, your streak and your current book to friends, for example on WhatsApp.
